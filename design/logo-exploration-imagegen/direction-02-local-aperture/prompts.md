# Direction 02 — Local Aperture

## Production method

- Ideation engine: built-in `image_gen`, one independent generation call per concept.
- Finalization: raw image generations were treated only as structural sketches. Their gradients, lighting, dirty alpha, or literal checkerboard pixels were rejected. Each accepted idea was redrawn as a self-contained production SVG and exported to PNG with `rsvg-convert`.
- Shared SVG contract: `viewBox="0 0 512 512"`; transparent background; logical group `id="mark"`; only path, rect, and polygon geometry; uniform flat fills `#182133` and `#5368E8`; no text, filters, gradients, shadows, external images, fonts, or `<use>` references; primary geometry remains inside the 72–440 safety region; narrow structural features are at least 36 units.

## d02-01 — Loopback Aperture

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: Token Station app logo concept
Primary request: Build a heavy asymmetric non-complete near-round shell with one broad off-center aperture. A single thick negative-space channel enters through that aperture, folds around a stable solid core, and returns toward the same side without an arrow or closed ring.
Style: strict Swiss-wayfinding flat vector pictogram; machine-cut and compact.
Composition: one centered wordless mark, true transparent background, 1:1, 15% safety margin, 32px-legible silhouette.
Palette: uniform ink #182133 with one short integrated signal-indigo #5368E8 face; negative space remains transparent.
Constraints: SVG-friendly, one-color convertible, no text, gradients, shadows, 3D, mockup, thin lines, dots, nodes, sparkles, train, tile, or decorative detail.
Avoid: complete C/ring, refresh/sync/power icon, arrowhead, eye, shield, house, lock, keyhole, camera aperture, spiral, letter G/Q, or competitor resemblance.
```

Acceptance: the flattened open shell and central tongue create one hairpin corridor; the silhouette is neither a circular refresh mark nor an eye, and all exterior/interior voids are genuine alpha.

There is no link, double loop, S, infinity, or refresh topology.
