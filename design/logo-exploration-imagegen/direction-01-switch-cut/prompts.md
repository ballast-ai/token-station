# Direction 01 — Switch Cut

Token Station is a local-first desktop AI request router. These five marks inherit only the useful semantic core of the existing identity: a dark oblique channel, one vermilion selection signal, and a decisive lateral switching action. They intentionally discard the old blue/purple SaaS tile, gradients, route diagrams, dots, and railway illustration.

## Production method

- Ideation: OpenAI built-in `image_gen`, one independent call per concept. Raw generations were treated only as shape studies because the raster outputs introduced forbidden gradients and lighting.
- Production: the useful structural idea from each study was redrawn as a clean, self-contained SVG.
- Export: `rsvg-convert -w 1024 -h 1024` produced the matching PNG.
- Final SVG contract: `viewBox="0 0 512 512"`, transparent background, `id="mark"`, no text, no external assets, solid `#15181B` and `#F04B2F` fills only.

## 01 Relay Slash

### Image-generation prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: Token Station app logo concept — second structural refinement of Relay Slash
Primary request: Fix the remaining lightning/Z reading. Replace the current top-and-bottom zigzag arrangement with two separate parallel slash-shaped ink beams placed side-by-side, both spanning the same vertical band and both leaning upward to the right. Bridge only their inner midsections with one short, perfectly horizontal vermilion rectangular switching bar. It should read as an abstract relay or two channels being cross-selected, never as a continuous zigzag, bolt, or road.
Style/medium: pure SVG-like flat vector logo mark; crisp hard-edged geometric solids; no tonal variation anywhere
Composition/framing: exactly one compact centered mark on true transparent 1:1 canvas; 15% safety margin; balanced near-square footprint; large negative spaces; bold 32 px silhouette
Color palette: every ink pixel is uniform #15181B; every accent pixel is uniform #F04B2F; no other visible color
Constraints: keep only two diagonal ink pieces and one horizontal vermilion piece; no touching outer endpoints; no text, typography, background, border, container, mockup; strictly flat fills with zero gradients, shading, highlight, texture, shadow, blur, glow, bevel or depth
Avoid: lightning bolt, Z, S, arrow, railway or traffic symbol, blue/purple, thin lines, dots, nodes, networks, sparkles, hexagons, shields, houses, infinity loops, coins, OpenRouter/CC Switch/OpenAI/Anthropic similarity, watermark
```

Production cleanup: two unequal offset slash beams are interrupted by a split vermilion switching bar; the central cut makes the latent T more explicit and prevents a continuous Z silhouette.

Acceptance: transparent, two flat colors, three major components, no gradients/text/container; the 64 px crossbar and thick beams survive 32 px.

## 02 Switch Wedge

### Image-generation prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: Token Station app logo concept — single isolated symbol, not a sheet
Primary request: Create an original “Switch Wedge” mark for Token Station, a local-first desktop AI request router. Abstract one input becoming two possible outputs using exactly three massive interlocking geometric wedge blocks: one blunt ink input wedge enters from the left, while two separated output wedges fan toward upper-right and lower-right. Make the upper-right selected wedge vermilion and the other two blocks ink. A single broad curved central cut between the blocks should suggest the momentum of an S without drawing a letter, arrow, railway track, or fork diagram. Outer ends must be blunt, not pointed arrowheads.
Style/medium: ultra-flat vector brand mark; screen-print-like solid shapes; hard crisp edges; sophisticated editorial geometry; compact and ownable, not a generic UI glyph
Composition/framing: exactly one centered near-square emblem on a genuinely transparent 1:1 canvas; 15% safety margin; three large pieces only; bold, asymmetric silhouette; large negative cut; legible at 32 px
Color palette: uniform solid ink #15181B and uniform solid vermilion #F04B2F only
Constraints: exactly three visible solid-color wedge pieces; no text or literal letters; actual alpha transparency; every colored region must be perfectly uniform with no tonal change; no gradients, shadows, glow, lighting, texture, bevel, depth, antialias halo, 3D, mockup, container, tile or border
Avoid: any resemblance to the Relay Slash/H/zigzag concept; lightning bolt, Z, arrow, road sign, railway tracks, crypto or traffic app; blue/purple; thin lines, circles, dots, nodes, network graph, AI sparkles, hexagon, shield, house, infinity sign, OpenRouter/CC Switch/OpenAI/Anthropic similarity, watermark
```

Production cleanup: the generated three-blade idea was rebuilt as three blunt, separated polygons with one vermilion selected wedge and a broad central S-turn aperture.

Acceptance: transparent, exactly three flat wedges, no pointed arrowhead, no rail line, no gradients/text/container; all pieces remain substantial at 32 px.

## 04 Crossing Gate

### Image-generation prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: Token Station app logo concept — single isolated symbol, not a sheet
Primary request: Create an original “Crossing Gate” mark for Token Station, a local-first desktop AI request router. Show the instant of a routing decision as an asymmetric crossing made from massive flat bands: one continuous ink diagonal band passes cleanly from lower-left to upper-right; a second shorter ink diagonal approaches from upper-left but ends in a precise squared stop before the crossing; one wide vermilion horizontal latch sits across that stopped endpoint, clearly blocking the rejected route while leaving the continuous route visually uninterrupted. Design the overlap with deliberate negative-space cuts, not outlines.
Style/medium: ultra-flat hard-edge vector brand mark; rigorous modernist construction; bold industrial editorial identity; solid geometric cut-paper shapes
Composition/framing: exactly one centered compact emblem on a genuinely transparent square canvas; 15% safety margin; asymmetric crossing footprint; strong silhouette and large apertures; readable at 32 px
Color palette: uniform solid ink #15181B and uniform solid vermilion #F04B2F only
Constraints: only large thick pieces; no text or letters; actual alpha transparency; each visible color must be perfectly solid and uniform; no gradient, lighting, shading, texture, shadow, glow, bevel, depth, 3D, mockup, background tile, border or container
Avoid: resemblance to Relay Slash parallel beams, Switch Wedge three-way fan, TS Cut block monogram; lightning bolt, Z, arrows, road/rail/traffic icon, crossed swords, crypto; blue/purple; thin lines, dots, nodes, networks, AI sparkles, hexagons, shields, houses, infinity signs, coins; OpenRouter/CC Switch/OpenAI/Anthropic similarity; watermark
```

Production cleanup: the raw Y-like branch was discarded. The final is an unequal-weight X crossing: the dominant channel passes through, the secondary channel is visibly cut into two square-ended pieces, and the vermilion latch intercepts only that secondary route.

Acceptance: transparent, two unequal dark channels plus one flat latch, no Y fork or arrow ends, no gradients/text/container; the secondary channel is 48 px wide in the 512 master.

Color palette: uniform solid ink #15181B and uniform solid vermilion #F04B2F only
Constraints: three short diagonal ink pieces plus one wide horizontal vermilion band; no text or typography; actual alpha transparency; solid uniform fills only, hard vector edges, no tonal variation; no gradients, light, shading, texture, shadow, glow, blur, bevel, depth, 3D, mockup, tile, border or container
Avoid: resemblance to Relay Slash two tall parallel beams, Switch Wedge three-way fan, TS Cut monogram blocks, Crossing Gate X/Y shape; lightning, Z, arrows, train/rail/road/traffic sign, crypto; blue/purple; thin lines, dots, nodes, network graphs, AI sparkles, hexagons, shields, houses, infinity signs, coins; OpenRouter/CC Switch/OpenAI/Anthropic similarity; watermark
