# Token Station — Shortlist decision

## Decision

No concept in this exploration should be shipped unchanged. The purpose of the set is to find a defensible mother shape; the next pass should refine only the strongest two rather than continue polishing all 25.

Two independent reviews disagreed at the top:

- the product/semantic scorecard ranked **D03-05 Fallback Switch** first;
- the competitor-distance and visual-identity review ranked **D05-05 Quiet Beacon** first.

My final design judgment is **D05-05 Quiet Beacon as Visual Champion**, with **D03-05 Fallback Switch as Semantic Champion**. The original problem was not missing product metaphors—it was that the previous set looked generic and visually unresolved. D05-05 has the strongest self-contained app-mark silhouette and the clearest distance from the current router/gateway category. D03-05 communicates failover more directly, but its pixel-step shape is easier to mistake for a generic `F`, pipe, seat, or UI glyph.

## Final five

| Rank | Candidate | Role | Why it survives | Main risk before adoption |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [D05-05 Quiet Beacon](./direction-05-signal-control/d05-05-quiet-beacon.svg) | **Visual Champion** | Near-square, original silhouette; strongest light/dark app-icon presence; survives 16–32 px. | Can read as abstract `B/8`, cassette, or ticket device; product meaning needs a more deliberate negative-space relationship. |
| 2 | [D03-05 Fallback Switch](./direction-03-dual-track-exchange/d03-05-fallback-switch.svg) | **Semantic Champion** | Most direct primary/fallback-route idea; compact and highly economical. | Central aperture becomes fragile at 16 px; risks `F`, stair, seat, or generic pixel-pipe readings. |
| 3 | [D05-04 Relay Flag](./direction-05-signal-control/d05-04-relay-flag.svg) | Industrial control | Open, asymmetric and far from common AI-logo clichés; reads like a physical control event. | Can drift toward `E/h`, a rune, antenna, or railway signal arm. |
| 4 | [D04-01 Perforated Unit](./direction-04-token-rack/d04-01-perforated-unit.svg) | Infrastructure | Strong data-unit mass and a single selected slot; excellent small-size survival. | Can become `2/Z/E` or an equipment/ticket silhouette, and its block mass approaches an app-tile shape. |
| 5 | [D01-05 Signal Stitch](./direction-01-switch-cut/d01-05-signal-stitch.svg) | Migration path | Retains the existing ink/vermilion routing DNA without repeating the old diagrammatic network style. | Too horizontal for some Dock contexts; rail, speed-line, cable, or lightning associations remain possible. |

## Do not advance

- **D03-01 / D03-02** — centre-oriented four-way constructions are too close to CC Switch's visual territory and generic expand/sync UI icons.
- **D02-01** — collapses into a generic `G/Q` or loopback/power mark.
- **D02-05** — chevron/portal/arrow readings are crowded by Portkey and other gateway brands.
- **D04-03** — breaks into barcode/chart/pause fragments at 16 px.

## Recommended refinement round

Develop three controlled variants each for **D05-05** and **D03-05** only:

1. enlarge or simplify the critical negative spaces for 16 px;
2. test one-colour tray silhouettes before judging colour;
3. remove the `B/8` tendency from D05-05 and the `F/stair` tendency from D03-05;
4. run an unlabelled five-second association test, then perform formal trademark similarity screening on the winner.

The current reviews are design screening, not a legal trademark clearance or user-recognition study.
